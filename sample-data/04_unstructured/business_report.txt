QUARTERLY BUSINESS PERFORMANCE REPORT
Q2 2024 — Acme Corporation

This report presents the financial performance of Acme Corporation for the second quarter of fiscal year 2024. Revenue reached $4.2 million, representing a 15% increase compared to the same period last year. The strong growth was primarily driven by excellent performance in the Enterprise segment, which contributed $2.8 million in revenue.

Operating expenses totalled $3.1 million, with marketing spend increasing by 22% to support the new product launch. Despite higher expenses, operating margins improved to 26%, up from 23% in Q2 2023. The company ended the quarter with $12.5 million in cash reserves and zero outstanding debt.

Customer acquisition costs decreased to $340 per customer, down 18% year-over-year. The total customer base grew to 18,420 active accounts, with a monthly churn rate of just 2.1%. Net Promoter Score reached 67, indicating strong customer satisfaction.

Looking forward, the company projects Q3 revenue between $4.8 million and $5.2 million, supported by the launch of three new product lines and expansion into the European market. Management has approved an additional $1.5 million in R&D investment for the second half of the year.

Key risks identified include potential supply chain disruptions affecting hardware availability, increasing competition in the mid-market segment, and regulatory changes in the European Union that may impact the planned expansion. The risk committee has developed mitigation strategies for each identified risk.

The board of directors has approved a quarterly dividend of $0.15 per share, payable on August 15, 2024 to shareholders of record as of July 31, 2024. The total dividend payout will be approximately $1.2 million based on the current share count of 8 million outstanding shares.
